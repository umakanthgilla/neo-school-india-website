USHA ONE Technologies Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 September 2026

USHA ONE Technologies provides business software, CRM, communication, automation and AI-enabled workflow services. This policy explains how information is handled when businesses and their customers use our services, including integrations with WhatsApp and Instagram.

Information we process

Depending on the service, we may process business contact details, customer names and contact information, messages and conversation metadata, lead and CRM information, workflow records, account identifiers, and technical information required to operate and secure the service.

How we use information

We use information to provide requested services, route and manage customer communications, maintain CRM records, support follow-ups and tasks, operate approved automations, improve reliability and security, and comply with applicable legal obligations. AI-assisted features may use relevant conversation and business context to prepare suggestions or perform configured workflows. High-trust actions may require human approval.

Third-party platforms

Our services may integrate with third-party platforms such as Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and Instagram APIs, hosting, database, automation and AI providers. Information is shared with such providers only as needed to provide configured services and is also subject to their applicable terms and policies.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including access controls and server-side handling of sensitive credentials. We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, legitimate business requirements, security, dispute resolution and legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law and the relationship through which your information was collected, you may request access, correction or deletion of personal information. Where USHA ONE processes information for a business customer, requests may need to be handled through that business.

Data deletion

Instructions for requesting deletion are available on our User Data Deletion page.

Changes

We may update this policy as our services or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are published.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact USHA ONE Technologies at ushaone4u@gmail.com.
