USHA ONE Technologies — User Data Deletion

Updated: 10 September 2026

You may request deletion of personal information associated with USHA ONE Technologies services, subject to applicable law, contractual obligations and records we must retain for legitimate legal, security or accounting purposes.

How to request deletion

Send an email to ushaone4u@gmail.com with the subject User Data Deletion Request. Include enough information for us to identify the relevant account or records, such as the business/service used and the email address or phone number associated with the interaction. Do not send passwords, access tokens or other authentication secrets.

What happens next

We may ask for reasonable verification to protect against unauthorized deletion. After validation, we will delete or anonymize data under our control that is eligible for deletion and will communicate the outcome. If we process the information on behalf of a business customer, we may direct the request to that business or coordinate with it as appropriate.

Third-party data

Deleting data from USHA ONE systems does not automatically delete information independently held by third-party platforms. You may need to use the relevant third party's own account or privacy controls for those records.

Contact

For questions about a deletion request, contact ushaone4u@gmail.com.
