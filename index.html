THE NEO WAY

Where Children Love to Learn

At Neo School India, learning is designed around the child — not around pressure, rote learning or memorisation.

Children explore, experience and understand concepts through meaningful activities, movement, communication and play.

Our approach supports language development, phonics, pre-math concepts, fine motor skills, gross motor development, social-emotional growth and confident communication.
